Choosing the right sofa fabric for Lagos humidity

August 3, 2026

Humidity changes everything about how upholstery ages. Velvet and heavy linen hold moisture longer than performance weaves, which matters more in Lagos than in a showroom in London.

We recommend a tightly woven polyester-blend velvet for statement pieces, and a treated leather or leatherette for anything near a window.
